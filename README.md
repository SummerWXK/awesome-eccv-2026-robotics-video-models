## Overview

This repository curates model-centric papers from the [official ECCV 2026 accepted-paper list](https://eccv.ecva.net/Conferences/2026/AcceptedPapers), with a focus on robotics, world models, world-action models, vision-language-action models, and generative video models.

### 🌟 Key Features

* **Structured taxonomy:** four focused tracks covering world-action models, world models, VLA models, and video generation/simulation.
* **Official conference metadata:** titles, first authors, sessions, and poster numbers are checked against ECCV/ECVA.
* **Verified paper links:** direct arXiv badges use exact-title matches or manually verified title variants; unresolved papers retain exact-title search badges.
* **Focused scope:** generic video understanding and representation-learning papers are intentionally excluded.

## 5. Scope and Verification

- **Official source:** ECCV 2026 Accepted Papers, retrieved on **2026-08-19 (Asia/Shanghai)**.
- **Conference snapshot:** 2814 accepted-paper rows were parsed from the official page; 228 model-centric papers were retained here.
- **Inclusion:** title-level relevance to world/world-action models, VLA, robot policies closely coupled to those models, or video generation/simulation. General video understanding and representation-learning papers are excluded.
- **Deduplication:** overlapping papers are assigned to one primary section in this order: world-action model → VLA → world model → video generation.
- **Link verification:** direct arXiv links require a normalized exact-title match or a manually verified title variant. LaMP (`Policy`/`Policies`) and ChronoFlow-Policy (expanded arXiv subtitle) are the two documented variants; search links are not presented as confirmed paper pages.
- **Interpretation boundary:** inclusion is based on the official title/category and, where available, arXiv metadata. It does not imply that every paper releases code or qualifies as a general-purpose foundation model.
