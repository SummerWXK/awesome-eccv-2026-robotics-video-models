## Scope and verification

- **Official source:** ECCV 2026 Accepted Papers, retrieved on **2026-08-19 (Asia/Shanghai)**.
- **Conference snapshot:** 2814 accepted-paper rows were parsed from the official page; 227 model-centric papers were retained here.
- **Inclusion:** title-level relevance to world/world-action models, VLA, robot policies closely coupled to those models, or video generation/simulation. General video understanding and representation-learning papers are excluded.
- **Deduplication:** overlapping papers are assigned to one primary section in this order: world-action model → VLA → world model → video generation.
- **Link verification:** direct arXiv links require a normalized exact-title match; search links are deliberately not presented as confirmed paper pages.
- **Interpretation boundary:** inclusion is based on the official title/category and, where available, arXiv metadata. It does not imply that every paper releases code or qualifies as a general-purpose foundation model.
