# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep this list accurate.

## Add a paper

Please confirm that the title appears on the [official ECCV 2026 accepted-paper list](https://eccv.ecva.net/Conferences/2026/AcceptedPapers), then add it to exactly one primary section:

1. World-Action Models
2. World Models
3. Vision-Language-Action Models
4. Video Generation & Simulation

Use the existing entry format:

```markdown
- 📄 [Exact official title](https://arxiv.org/abs/YYMM.NNNNN) — First Author et al. · Poster S# · ##
```

Use `📄` only for a direct paper page whose title matches the official title. If no paper page is available, use `🔎` and an exact-title arXiv search link. Do not infer a link from a similar title.

## Correct metadata

For title, author, session, or poster-number corrections, cite the official ECCV page. For arXiv, project-page, or code-link corrections, include the destination URL and verify that its title and authors match.

Keep entries alphabetical within each section and avoid adding the same paper to multiple sections.
